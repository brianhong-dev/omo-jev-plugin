# Code of Conduct

We want a welcoming, constructive space for everyone participating in this project.

Be respectful of different experiences and viewpoints. Discuss ideas and code, not personal characteristics. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately by contacting [the maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/brianhong-dev) rather than opening a public issue. Maintainers will review reports and respond as they are able.
